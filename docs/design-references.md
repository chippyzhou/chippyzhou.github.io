# Design references

## Long-form reading: Joe Carlsmith

Reference screenshot: [joe-carlsmith-longform-layout.png](design-references/joe-carlsmith-longform-layout.png)

Source visible in the reference:
`https://joecarlsmith.com/2026/03/19/on-restraining-ai-development-for-the-sake-of-safety/`

Use this direction for future technical-note and long-form article reading
views, without replacing the portfolio homepage or the private-space visual
identity.

### Visual language

- White editorial canvas with unusually generous negative space.
- Classical serif typography for the site name, article title, body, and
  navigation.
- Thin black horizontal rules instead of cards, shadows, or decorative
  containers.
- Restrained palette: black text, soft gray secondary text, and one clear blue
  for links and active references.
- Wide desktop composition with a persistent left table of contents and a
  narrower central reading column.
- Large figures sit directly in the article flow with no ornamental frame.
- Hierarchy comes from scale, spacing, indentation, and italics rather than
  colored panels.

### Interaction and responsive notes

- Desktop: sticky article identity and table of contents on the left; reading
  content remains the dominant area.
- Mobile: collapse the table of contents into a compact disclosure above the
  article and keep the body as a single readable column.
- Preserve native-feeling link treatment and quiet hover states.
- Do not copy the layout literally. Blend its reading discipline with the
  site's girl-band editorial motifs through small annotations, track metadata,
  and the private-space music player.
